# Customer Churn Analysis

## AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship

### Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn for ABC Communications Ltd. using the Telco Customer Churn dataset. The objective is to identify the factors influencing customer churn and provide actionable business recommendations that improve customer retention.

---

## Business Objectives

- Understand the customer base.
- Identify customer segments with the highest churn.
- Evaluate the impact of contract type on customer retention.
- Analyze the relationship between customer tenure and loyalty.
- Identify services associated with customer churn.
- Examine payment methods influencing churn.
- Provide business recommendations.

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Telco Customer Churn Dataset (Kaggle)

## Key Findings

- Month-to-month customers have the highest churn.
- Short-tenure customers are more likely to leave.
- Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
- Technical support contributes to customer retention.
- Long-term contracts improve customer loyalty.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Promote long-term contracts.
- Improve onboarding for new customers.
- Bundle support services.
- Review pricing strategies.
- Encourage automatic payment methods.
